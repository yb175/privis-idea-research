# SIH slide copy

Max 5 bullets per slide. Max 12 words per bullet.

Slide 2: 3 problem + 3 solution + Today vs PRIVIS table.
Slide 3: architecture + caption raw screenshot does not leave before Policy Gate.
Slide 4: feasible why + 5 risks with fixes.
Slide 5: users + 25/20/20/20/15 metrics.
Slide 6: 6-8 named sources.
